Connection sound …

A short sound, never longer than 2 seconds, that customers hear on their own phone the moment they get connected: after entering a voucher, finishing a payment, or reconnecting. It does not play when someone just reopens the page while already connected.

Your own sound (optional)

MP3, WAV, OGG or M4A, up to 300 KB. Only the first 2 seconds are ever played, so trim it to a short clip.

Phones only allow sound after the customer has tapped something on the page (that is a browser rule), and a phone on silent mode or with its volume down will not play it. If a browser blocks the sound, the customer is simply connected without it.

Connection

Create or manage your own account at sms.ots.co.ke. In the provider dashboard open Developers > Reveal token, then enter that token and your approved sender ID below. The sender ID may be an approved alphanumeric ID (up to 11 characters) or a phone number with country code. Press Save; the token is stored only in this computer's local settings, takes effect without a restart, and is never displayed, even partially, by this dashboard. Never paste it into the website, GitHub, Vercel, or support messages.

Use your own Courtney Tech account. Create or manage it at courtneytech.xyz, then open File > Edit settings (.env) and enter COURTNEYTECH_API_KEY, COURTNEYTECH_API_SECRET, and your numeric COURTNEYTECH_ACCOUNT_ID.
