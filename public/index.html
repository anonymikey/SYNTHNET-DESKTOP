📺 Add a TV by its MAC address

For a TV, game console or streaming stick that cannot open the sign-in page. Enter its Wi-Fi (MAC) address once and it connects by itself, with no sign-in. When your package ends the TV is switched off too, and it switches back on automatically when you buy your next package.

How do I find my TV's MAC address?
  • Samsung: Settings > General > Network > Network Status, or Support > About this TV > Wi-Fi MAC address.
  • LG: Settings > All Settings > Connection > Wi-Fi Connection > Advanced Wi-Fi Settings, or General > About this TV.
  • Android TV / Google TV: Settings > Device Preferences (or System) > About > Status > Wi-Fi MAC address.
  • Fire TV: Settings > My Fire TV > About > Network. Roku: Settings > Network > About.
  • Anything else: look in Network, About or Status for "Wi-Fi MAC" or "Wireless MAC". Use the Wi-Fi address, not the Ethernet one.
  • If your TV has a "Private Wi-Fi address" or "Random MAC" setting, switch it off (or choose "use device MAC") so the address never changes.
